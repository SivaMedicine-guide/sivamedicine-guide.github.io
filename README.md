# Medicine Guide by Age & Disease

For registered medical practitioners in Andhra Pradesh. Enter patient name, gender, age, weight, temperature, BP and sugar, then type symptoms (English or Telugu) or pick a disease from 20 body-part groups. The app decides severity and shows a prescription table (medicine, dose, timing, before/after food, days) with brand examples, combination and allergy warnings, and shares it on WhatsApp as an image, PDF or text. Works offline and installs on the phone.

Open the app: https://sivamedicine-guide.github.io/
